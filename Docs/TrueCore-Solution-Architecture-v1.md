Executive Summary

TrueCore Solutions Portal is a procurement operations platform designed to centralize and automate the municipal procurement lifecycle.

The system will manage:

Supplier Catalogues
Product Master Data
RFQs
Pricing Automation
Quote Generation
Project Delivery
Document Management
Reporting
AI-Assisted Procurement

The platform must support future scaling from a founder-operated business to a remote procurement team.
